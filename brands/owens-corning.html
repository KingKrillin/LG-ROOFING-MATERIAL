WHO IS OWENS CORNING

Owens Corning has been a household name in building materials for decades, known for pairing roofing systems with proprietary technology like SureNail®.

Their Duration series shingles are engineered to resist wind, algae, and granule loss, giving homeowners a long-lasting, low-maintenance roof.
