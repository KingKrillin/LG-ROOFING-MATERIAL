1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how LG Roofing Repairs and Materials Inc, doing business as LG Roofing Materials ("LG Roofing Materials," "we," "us," or "our"), a roofing materials dealer at 200 Bluebell Road, Houston, Texas 77037, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website (the "Site"), contact us, request an estimate, or receive text messages from us. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form, estimate tool, or quote request form, you may provide:

  • Your name, company name, mobile phone number, and email address;
  • A delivery or job-site address;
  • Your project details, such as the brands, products, and quantities you need, when you need them, material lists, photos, or files you upload, your pickup or delivery preference, and any message or notes you write;
  • Your choices about receiving text messages.

We also receive whatever information you choose to share when you call, text, or email us.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services embedded on the Site may automatically receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your questions and prepare quotes and estimates;
  • Process, schedule, and fulfill orders, pickups, and deliveries;
  • Contact you by phone, email, or text message about your inquiry, quote, order, or delivery, and, only if you separately agree, send you marketing text messages about specials and stock arrivals, as described in our Text Messaging Terms;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Keep the Site secure, prevent spam and fraud, and keep it working properly;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Text Messaging & Mobile Information

If you agree to receive text messages from LG Roofing Materials, we use your mobile number only to send the messages described in our Text Messaging Terms: quotes, order and delivery updates, and customer service replies, and marketing messages only if you separately agreed to them. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to run our business and serve you, with service providers that act on our behalf:

  • Formspree, which receives and delivers our contact and estimate form submissions to us;
  • Vercel, which hosts the Site, and Resend, which delivers a backup email copy of estimate requests to us;
  • Our customer management, phone, and text messaging providers, so we can keep track of your requests and call and text you;
  • Delivery partners, when you request delivery, so they can bring your order to the address you give us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business. Except as described in this policy, we do not share your personal information with third parties, and we never share mobile numbers or text messaging consent for anyone else's marketing.

6. Cookies & Third-Party Content

The Site does not use advertising cookies. Some pages include content from third parties that may set their own cookies or collect technical data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps, for the embedded map showing our location;
  • Google Fonts, for the fonts used on the Site;
  • Elfsight, for the customer reviews shown on our home page.

The Site also links to manufacturer websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party site, and we encourage you to read their policies.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, such as following up on your estimate, completing your order, keeping business and tax records, and meeting legal requirements. After that, we delete it or keep it only in a form that does not identify you.

8. How We Protect Information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, and the Site is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices

  • Text messages: Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts.
  • Email: Ask us at any time to stop emailing you.
  • Access, correction, and deletion: Contact us to ask what information we have about you, or to correct or delete it, subject to any records we must keep by law.

10. Children's Privacy

The Site is meant for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting the new version on this page and changing the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
