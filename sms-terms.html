1. Program Description

LG Roofing Materials sends text messages to customers and prospective customers who have agreed to receive them. Messages may include replies to your questions, quotes and estimates, order confirmations, pickup and delivery scheduling and updates, and other customer service messages about your order or inquiry.

2. How You Opt In

You may agree to receive text messages from LG Roofing Materials by giving us your mobile number and consent, for example when you request a quote or estimate, place an order, or text us first. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your inquiry and order activity.

4. Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan with your wireless carrier for details.

5. How to Opt Out

You can cancel text messages at any time. Reply STOP to any message from us. After you send STOP, we will send one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will not receive further text messages from us unless you opt in again. You can also reply CANCEL, END, QUIT, or UNSUBSCRIBE.

6. How to Get Help

For help, reply HELP to any message, call us at (832) 408-0872, or email sales@lgroofingmaterial.com.

7. Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery depends on your carrier and device and is not guaranteed.

8. Privacy

We respect your privacy. Your mobile number and consent are handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information-sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and the text messaging platform that delivers our messages, is permitted only to provide that service to you.

9. Changes to These Terms

We may update these Text Messaging Terms from time to time by posting the new version on this page and changing the "Last updated" date above.
